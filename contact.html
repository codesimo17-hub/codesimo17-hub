Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my social media accounts?

After creating an account, navigate to Settings > Connected Accounts and click "Connect" next to the platform you want to link. You'll be redirected to authorize access through the official platform OAuth flow.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transmission and store sensitive information encrypted at rest. We never store your social media passwords. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

What platforms do you support?

Currently, we support TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram, with more platforms coming soon. Check the homepage for the latest supported platforms.

Can I schedule posts in advance?

Yes! Our intelligent scheduling system allows you to queue videos and set custom publication times, frequencies, and target platforms.

How do I request TikTok API access?

Email us at support@videopublisher2311.com with "TikTok API Access Request" in the subject line. Include details about your use case and we'll guide you through the application process.

What if I need to delete my account?

You can delete your account anytime from Settings > Account > Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed within 30 days as per our data retention policy.
